Pipeline Booster Pump Solution

LOKJ pipeline booster pump solutions are designed for water pressure boosting in houses, apartments, villas, commercial buildings and small industrial water supply systems.

When the water pressure is low, unstable or unable to meet multiple water points at the same time, an automatic booster pump can help improve water flow and provide more stable pressure for daily water use.

LOKJ provides different booster pump models for household water supply, pipeline pressure increasing, hot water circulation, rooftop tank supply and building water pressure systems.

FAQ

What is a pipeline booster pump used for?

A pipeline booster pump is used to increase water pressure in household, commercial and building water supply systems.

When do I need a booster pump?

A booster pump is suitable when tap water pressure is low, water flow is unstable, or several faucets and showers need to work at the same time.

Can a booster pump start and stop automatically?

Yes. Automatic booster pumps can start when water is used and stop when water demand ends, helping provide convenient pressure boosting for daily water use.

Which pump is suitable for pipeline pressure boosting?

The suitable pump depends on flow rate, head, pipe diameter, water source and usage scenario. LOKJ can provide different booster pump solutions for household and commercial applications.
