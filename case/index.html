FAQ

What applications are water pumps used for?

Water pumps are widely used in agricultural irrigation, household water supply, industrial circulation systems, sewage treatment, drainage and pipeline pressure boosting applications.

How to choose the right pump for different applications?

The suitable pump depends on flow rate, head, water source, pipe diameter, working environment and application requirements.

Which pumps are suitable for agricultural irrigation?

Submersible pumps, centrifugal pumps and irrigation booster pumps are commonly used for farmland irrigation and agricultural water supply systems.

What pumps are suitable for household water supply?

Booster pumps, self-priming pumps and automatic water supply systems are widely used in residential water supply applications.

Can LOKJ provide solutions for industrial and sewage applications?

Yes. LOKJ provides industrial water pumps, sewage pumps, drainage systems and automatic control solutions for different working conditions.
