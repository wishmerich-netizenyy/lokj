FAQ

What pumps are suitable for farm irrigation?

Submersible pumps, centrifugal pumps and irrigation booster pumps are commonly used for agricultural irrigation and farmland watering systems.

Can irrigation pumps work continuously?

Yes. LOKJ irrigation pumps are designed for stable long-time operation in agricultural irrigation and rural water supply applications.

Where are agricultural water pumps used?

Agricultural water pumps are widely used in farmland irrigation, orchard watering, vegetable planting, sprinkler irrigation and rural water systems.

How to choose the right irrigation pump?

The suitable irrigation pump depends on water source, irrigation distance, flow rate, head and pipe diameter requirements.
