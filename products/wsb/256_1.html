WQ D Type Submersible Sewage Pump

LOKJ WQ D type submersible sewage pump is a general-purpose wastewater drainage pump for construction sites, industrial wastewater discharge, municipal drainage, agricultural drainage and residential sewage applications.

This series covers common models from 0.75KW to 7.5KW, with compact structure, stable operation and good passing capacity for sewage, rainwater and wastewater containing small solid particles or long fibers.

The WQ D type sewage pump is suitable for users who need a reliable, cost-effective and easy-to-maintain submersible pump for daily drainage and general sewage transfer projects.

产品详情

产品概况

绿欧科技(Lvou Technology) WQ系列D款潜水排污泵，是目前市场上应用最为广泛的通用型排污泵。该系列功率覆盖0.75KW至7.5KW，采用下吸式结构，能有效抽送含有固体颗粒和长纤维的污水、废水、雨水及城市生活污水。其叶轮流道设计科学，抗堵塞能力强，运行平稳可靠。


核心优势

  • 性价比高：经典的D款设计，技术成熟，维护成本低，性价比极高。
  • 运行稳定：电机与泵体同轴设计，同心度高，振动小，噪音低。
  • 双重密封：采用优质机械密封，耐腐蚀、耐磨损，有效防止电机进水。
  • 应用广泛：适用于建筑工地排水、农业排灌、企业废水排放及人防工程等。

FAQ

What is the WQ D type sewage pump used for?

It is used for construction site drainage, industrial wastewater discharge, municipal drainage, agricultural drainage, rainwater drainage and general sewage transfer applications.

What is the power range of this WQ D type pump?

This page mainly covers regular WQ D type sewage pump models from 0.75KW to 7.5KW for common drainage applications.

Can this pump handle sewage with particles?

Yes. The pump is designed for wastewater containing small solid particles or soft impurities, but it should not be used for oversized hard solids or highly corrosive liquids without material confirmation.

How to choose the right WQ D type sewage pump?

The model should be selected according to required flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, voltage, sewage concentration, particle condition and drainage distance.
