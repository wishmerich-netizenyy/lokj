WQ Large Submersible Sewage Pump

LOKJ WQ large submersible sewage pump is designed for municipal sewage systems, large pump stations, industrial wastewater discharge, mine drainage, construction site drainage and heavy-duty sewage transfer projects.

The series covers high-power models for large flow and high head applications. With a wide-channel impeller and heavy-duty cast iron structure, the pump can handle sewage containing solid particles, long fibers and suspended impurities.

It supports flange connection and automatic coupling installation, making maintenance more convenient for sewage pits, municipal pipe networks and industrial wastewater treatment systems.

产品详情

产品概况

绿欧科技(Lvou Technology) WQ系列大功率潜水排污泵，是针对市政管网、大型泵站及工业重污染废水排放而研发的高性能产品。功率覆盖11KW至550KW，具有显著的节能效果、防缠绕、无堵塞等特点。该系列水泵采用独特的水力设计，叶轮流道宽大，通过性能极强，能有效输送含有固体颗粒和长纤维的污水。


核心优势

  • 重工品质：采用高强度铸铁泵体，结构坚固，运行稳定，抗震性能好。
  • 高效无堵塞：大流道叶轮设计，确保污物、杂质顺畅通过，大大减少堵塞风险。
  • 安全可靠：配备双重机械密封及油室漏水保护装置，可选配全自动控制柜，对过载、缺相、短路、漏水等进行全方位保护。
  • 安装灵活：支持自动耦合安装系统，安装维护极其方便，无需人员进入污水坑。

FAQ

What is the WQ large submersible sewage pump used for?

It is used for municipal sewage systems, large pump stations, industrial wastewater drainage, mine drainage, construction drainage and heavy-duty sewage transfer projects.

What are the advantages of a large WQ sewage pump?

Large WQ sewage pumps provide high flow capacity, strong passing ability, heavy-duty structure and stable operation for municipal and industrial wastewater systems.

Can this pump handle sewage with particles and fibers?

Yes. The wide-channel hydraulic design helps transfer sewage containing solid particles, long fibers and suspended impurities.

How to choose a suitable WQ large sewage pump?

The model should be selected according to flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, voltage, installation method, sewage concentration and pump station working conditions.
