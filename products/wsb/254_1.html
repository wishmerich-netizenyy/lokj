WQ / WQD Stainless Steel Submersible Sewage Pump

LOKJ WQ / WQD stainless steel submersible sewage pump is designed for wastewater drainage, corrosive liquid transfer, chemical wastewater discharge, food processing drainage, seawater aquaculture drainage and environmental protection projects.

Compared with traditional cast iron sewage pumps, the stainless steel structure provides better corrosion resistance, cleaner liquid transfer and improved appearance for applications with higher water quality or anti-rust requirements.

This series is suitable for factories, commercial buildings, sewage stations, food processing plants, chemical wastewater systems and special drainage environments.

产品详情

产品概况

绿欧科技(Lvou Technology) WQ/WQD-A系列是一款高端不锈钢潜水排污泵。区别于传统铸铁泵，该系列主要部件采用不锈钢材质冲压成型，具有重量轻、外形美观、耐腐蚀性强等特点。是环保工业、食品加工、医药卫生等对水质要求较高或介质具有一定腐蚀性的场所的理想排水设备。


核心优势

  • 不锈钢机身：外壳及过流部件采用优质不锈钢材质，有效抵抗酸碱腐蚀，防止生锈污染水质。
  • 高效节能：采用水力模型设计，电机效率高，运行平稳，噪音低。
  • 适用广泛：不仅适用于一般污水排放，特别适用于化工废水、食品加工废水及海水养殖排水。
  • 精密密封：配备高品质机械密封和O型耐油橡胶密封圈，确保水泵长期安全运行。

FAQ

What is the WQ / WQD stainless steel sewage pump used for?

It is used for wastewater drainage, chemical wastewater discharge, food processing drainage, sewage treatment, seawater aquaculture drainage and corrosive liquid transfer applications.

What is the advantage of a stainless steel sewage pump?

Stainless steel sewage pumps offer better corrosion resistance, cleaner liquid transfer, anti-rust performance and longer service life in humid or mildly corrosive environments.

Can this pump be used for chemical wastewater?

It can be used for some mildly corrosive wastewater applications, but the liquid composition, pH value and material compatibility should be confirmed before selection.

How to choose the right stainless steel sewage pump?

The model should be selected according to flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, voltage, liquid type, pH value, particle condition and drainage distance.
