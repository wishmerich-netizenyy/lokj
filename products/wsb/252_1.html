WQD Cast Iron Submersible Sewage Pump

LOKJ WQD cast iron submersible sewage pump is designed for wastewater drainage, industrial sewage discharge, construction site drainage, farm drainage and municipal environmental engineering applications.

The cast iron pump body provides strong durability for harsh working environments, while the sewage pump structure helps transfer wastewater containing small particles or soft impurities.

This series is suitable for users who need reliable drainage performance, strong structure and stable operation for sewage water, dirty water and wastewater transfer.

FAQ

What is the WQD cast iron sewage pump used for?

It is used for wastewater drainage, construction site drainage, industrial sewage discharge, farm drainage, municipal engineering and dirty water transfer applications.

Can the WQD pump handle dirty water?

Yes. The WQD series is designed for sewage water and dirty water, and can handle wastewater containing small particles or soft impurities.

What is the advantage of a cast iron sewage pump?

Cast iron pumps provide strong mechanical strength and durability, making them suitable for construction sites, industrial drainage and harsh working environments.

How to choose a suitable WQD sewage pump?

The model should be selected according to flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, water quality, particle condition, installation depth and drainage distance.
