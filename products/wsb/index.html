Submersible Sewage Pump and Wastewater Pump Products

FAQ

What sewage pump products are included in this category?

This category includes WQ large sewage pumps, WQC cutting sewage pumps, WQ D type sewage pumps, WQ / WQD stainless steel sewage pumps and WQD cast iron sewage pumps.

What is a submersible sewage pump used for?

A submersible sewage pump is used for wastewater drainage, municipal sewage systems, construction site drainage, septic tanks, industrial wastewater and dirty water transfer.

What is the difference between WQ, WQD and WQC sewage pumps?

WQ models are commonly three-phase sewage pumps, WQD models are usually single-phase sewage pumps, and WQC models include a cutting structure for sewage containing fibers or soft impurities.

How to choose a suitable sewage pump?

The pump should be selected according to required flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, voltage, sewage type, particle size, cable length and installation method.
