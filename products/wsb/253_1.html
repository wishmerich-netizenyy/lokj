WQC Cutting Submersible Sewage Pump

LOKJ WQC cutting submersible sewage pump is designed for septic tank drainage, livestock farm sewage discharge, construction slurry pumping, biogas tank residue drainage and wastewater containing fibers or soft impurities.

The pump is equipped with a high-hardness cutting blade that can cut long fibers, plastic bags, straw, cloth strips and other soft impurities before they enter the impeller, helping reduce clogging and winding problems.

Some models also include an agitation function, which helps stir settled sludge and discharge it together with wastewater for easier sewage handling.

产品详情

产品概况

绿欧科技(Lvou Technology) WQC系列切割式排污泵，是在普通排污泵的基础上，针对化粪池清理、养殖场排污等容易堵塞的场景特别研发的。泵体底部装有旋转切割刀头，能在吸入污物前将长纤维、塑料袋、稻草、布条等杂质切碎，彻底解决了水泵叶轮被缠绕、卡死的问题。


核心卖点

  • 强力切割：高硬度合金刀盘，锋利耐磨，快速切断杂质。
  • 双重功能：部分型号（搅匀款）配备副叶轮，能自动搅匀池底沉淀物（如泥沙、粪便），随水排出，无需人工清理。
  • 过流能力强：大流道设计，配合切割功能，排污更顺畅。
  • 安全耐用：内置热保护器，缺水或过载时自动停机保护电机。

FAQ

What is the WQC cutting sewage pump used for?

It is used for septic tank drainage, livestock farm sewage discharge, construction slurry pumping, biogas tank residue drainage and wastewater containing fibers or soft impurities.

What is the advantage of a cutting sewage pump?

A cutting sewage pump can cut soft impurities before they enter the impeller, helping reduce clogging, winding and pump blockage during sewage drainage.

Where can WQC cutting pumps be installed?

They are suitable for septic tanks, farms, basements, construction sites, sewage pits, sludge tanks and other wastewater drainage environments.

How to choose a suitable WQC cutting sewage pump?

The model should be selected according to flow rate, head, outlet diameter, power, voltage, sewage concentration, impurity condition and drainage distance.
