ZWS-W Stainless Steel Vortex Self-priming Pump

LOKJ ZWS-W stainless steel vortex self-priming pump is designed for household water pressure boosting, small flow high head applications, clean water transfer and gas-liquid mixed delivery.

The vortex impeller structure provides stable pressure in low-flow applications, making it suitable for apartments, villas, hot water systems and pipeline terminal boosting.

With stainless steel hydraulic components, compact design and automatic control options, this pump is suitable for clean water systems where corrosion resistance and stable operation are required.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 ZWS-W-BP 系列不锈钢漩涡式自动自吸泵，专为小流量、高扬程应用场景设计。该系列水泵采用漩涡式叶轮结构，具备优异的气液混合输送能力。泵体采用优质不锈钢制造，确保水质清洁卫生，特别适用于家庭自来水增压、热水器增压以及对水质要求较高的工业微量输送场合。

产品特点

  • 气液混合输送：独特的漩涡结构设计，允许输送含有少量气体的液体，运行稳定。
  • 小流量高扬程：在较小的流量下能提供较高的压力，非常适合管道末端增压。
  • 不锈钢材质：过流部件采用不锈钢制作，耐腐蚀，不生锈，保证用水安全。
  • 自动控制：（如选配自动款）配备自动开关控制，用水即开，停水即停，使用便捷。
  • 结构紧凑：体积小巧，安装方便，节省空间。

应用领域

  • 家庭增压：公寓、别墅的自来水管道增压，热水器水压不足增压。
  • 工业应用：锅炉给水、冷凝水回收、机床冷却系统。
  • 特殊输送：适用于输送挥发性液体或含有气体的液体。

使用条件

  • 介质要求：清水或物理化学性质类似于清水的液体，无固体颗粒。
  • 介质温度：-15℃ 至 +80℃（具体视密封材料而定）。
  • 环境温度：不超过 +40℃。
  • 安装要求：安装在通风良好、无雨淋的室内或遮蔽处。

FAQ

What is the ZWS-W stainless steel vortex pump used for?

It is used for household water pressure boosting, clean water transfer, hot water system boosting, small flow high head applications and gas-liquid mixed delivery.

What are the advantages of a vortex self-priming pump?

A vortex self-priming pump can provide stable pressure under small flow conditions and is suitable for pipeline terminal boosting and compact water supply systems.

Is this pump suitable for dirty water?

No. This pump is suitable for clean water or liquids similar to clean water, without solid particles, long fibers or abrasive impurities.

How to choose a suitable ZWS-W pump model?

The model should be selected according to required flow rate, head, suction condition, power, pipe diameter, voltage and actual water usage environment.
