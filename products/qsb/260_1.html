QDX-A Stainless Steel Submersible Pump

LOKJ QDX-A stainless steel submersible pump is designed for clean water transfer, household water supply, fish farming, garden irrigation and agricultural applications.

The pump adopts stainless steel housing with excellent corrosion resistance, lightweight structure and long service life. Compared with ordinary cast iron pumps, stainless steel pumps are cleaner, more durable and better suited for humid environments.

QDX-A and QX-A series are widely used for fish ponds, wells, water towers, farmland irrigation, landscape watering and domestic water systems.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 QDX-A / QX-A 系列不锈钢潜水电泵是在常规 QDX 系列基础上升级打造的精品水泵。其最大特点是采用不锈钢材质机筒，相比传统铸铝或铸铁机身，具有更强的耐腐蚀性和更精美的外观。该系列电泵由水泵、密封和电动机三部分组成，结构紧凑，重量轻，对水质无二次污染，是现代家庭生活用水、水产养殖及景观喷灌的理想选择。

产品特点

  • 不锈钢机筒设计：泵体外壳采用不锈钢材质，有效防止锈蚀，保持外观光亮如新，特别适合潮湿环境。
  • 耐腐蚀性能优越：相比普通材料，不锈钢外壳能更好地抵抗弱酸弱碱环境，延长泵体在恶劣水质中的使用寿命。
  • 绿色环保：不生锈材质避免了传统水泵生锈后对水源造成的二次污染，确保生活用水清洁。
  • 轻便耐用：在保证强度的同时，重量轻于铸铁泵，移动携带非常方便。
  • 安全可靠：内置热保护器（单相），双重机械密封，确保电机运行安全。

应用领域

  • 水产养殖：因其耐腐蚀特性，特别适合鱼塘、虾池的增氧与排水。
  • 家庭供水：井用提水、楼顶水塔送水，保证水质清洁。
  • 景观喷灌：花园浇水、园林喷泉。
  • 工业应用：轻微腐蚀性液体的排放（需确认PH值）。

使用条件

  • 介质要求：输送介质为清水，不含长纤维，PH值在 4-10 之间。
  • 含沙量：水中固体杂质体积比不超过0.1%，粒度不大于0.2mm。
  • 介质温度：输送介质温度不超过40℃。
  • 潜水深度：电泵潜入水下深度不超过5米。

FAQ

What is the QDX-A stainless steel submersible pump used for?

It is suitable for household water supply, fish farming, garden irrigation, farmland watering, well water lifting and clean water transfer applications.

What are the advantages of stainless steel submersible pumps?

Stainless steel pumps offer better corrosion resistance, cleaner water delivery, lighter weight and longer service life compared with ordinary cast iron pumps.

Can the QDX-A pump be used in fish ponds?

Yes. The stainless steel structure makes it suitable for aquaculture, fish ponds and other humid environments.

What is the difference between QDX-A and QX-A pumps?

QDX-A models are single-phase 220V pumps, while QX-A models are three-phase 380V pumps.
