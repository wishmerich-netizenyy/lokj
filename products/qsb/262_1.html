QD Multistage Oil-filled Submersible Pump

LOKJ QD multistage oil-filled submersible pump is designed for high head water lifting, farmland irrigation, well water pumping, garden watering, water tower supply and small industrial water transfer applications.

The pump uses a multistage centrifugal impeller structure to achieve higher head in a compact body. It is often called a “small mouse pump” because of its compact shape and convenient movement.

With oil-filled motor cooling, double mechanical sealing and compact design, the QD series provides reliable clean water pumping performance for irrigation, wells, rooftops and long-distance water delivery.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 QD 系列潜水电泵（Multi-stage Submersible Pump），因其体积小巧、形状独特，被行业内亲切地称为“小老鼠”泵。该泵由水泵、密封和电动机三部分组成，水泵位于电泵上部，采用多级离心式叶轮结构，能够在较小的体积下实现较高的扬程。电机位于下部，采用油浸式结构，散热效果极佳。该系列产品广泛应用于广大农村、工矿企业的提水工程。

产品特点

  • 高扬程设计：采用多级离心叶轮串联技术，相比同功率的普通潜水泵，QD系列的扬程更高，适合从较深的井中提水。
  • 油浸式电机：电机内部充满机械油，不仅润滑轴承，更重要的是能将电机产生的热量快速传导至泵壳，冷却效果好，支持长时间运行。
  • 体积小重量轻：结构设计紧凑，重量轻，移动非常方便，特别适合流动的灌溉作业。
  • 双重密封：采用双端面机械密封，有效防止水进入电机内部，提高了水泵的可靠性和耐用性。
  • 滤网防护：进水口设计有不锈钢或铸铁滤网，有效阻挡大颗粒杂质进入泵体，保护叶轮。

应用领域

  • 农田微灌：适合农田、菜园的喷灌和滴灌系统，压力足。
  • 井用提水：农村浅井、机井的生活用水提取。
  • 高层送水：利用其高扬程特性，可向楼顶水塔或高处水池送水。
  • 园林浇灌：公园、花园的远距离浇水。

使用条件

  • 介质要求：输送介质为清水，不含长纤维，含沙量（质量比）不超过0.01%。
  • 介质温度：输送介质温度不超过40℃。
  • PH值：输送液体PH值在 6.5 - 8.5 之间。
  • 潜水深度：电泵潜入水下深度不超过5米，且应距离水底50cm以上，防止吸入泥沙。

FAQ

What is the QD multistage submersible pump used for?

It is used for high head water lifting, farmland irrigation, garden watering, well water pumping, water tower supply and small industrial water transfer applications.

Why is the QD pump suitable for high head applications?

The QD pump uses multiple centrifugal impellers in series, allowing it to generate higher head than many ordinary submersible pumps with similar power.

Can the QD pump be used for dirty water?

No. The QD series is mainly suitable for clean water. It should not be used for water containing long fibers, heavy mud or large solid particles.

How to choose the right QD pump model?

The model should be selected according to required flow rate, total head, outlet diameter, voltage, power, submersion depth, pipe length and actual water lifting height.
