CDL / CDLF Vertical Multistage Centrifugal Pump

LOKJ CDL / CDLF vertical multistage centrifugal pump is designed for high-rise building water supply, industrial water treatment, boiler feed, firefighting systems and pressure boosting applications.

The vertical multistage structure provides high head performance with compact installation space, stable operation, low noise and efficient water transfer for different pressure requirements.

CDLF stainless steel models are suitable for clean water and light corrosive liquid applications, while CDL models provide reliable performance for general industrial and building water systems.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 CDL/CDLF 系列轻型立式多级离心泵，是一种非自吸立式多级离心泵，配有标准电机。电机轴通过联轴器直接与泵轴联接，拉杆螺栓将耐压筒、过流部件固定在泵头和进出水段之间。该泵具有体积小、重量轻、外形美观、效率高、噪音低等特点，其进出水口在同一水平线上（管道式结构），便于管路连接。

产品特点

  • 高效节能：采用优秀的水力模型设计，配合先进的制造工艺，大大提高了泵的效率和使用寿命。
  • 安装方便：进出水口位于泵底同一直线上，可直接安装在水平管路上。
  • 材质优良：CDLF系列过流部件均采用304或316不锈钢制成，具有良好的耐腐蚀性；CDL系列适用于无腐蚀性液体。
  • 运行可靠：采用集装式机械密封，维护方便，密封性能好，无泄漏。
  • 应用广泛：可输送从自来水到工业液体的各种不同介质，适应不同的温度、流量和压力范围。

应用领域

  • 供水：水厂过滤与输送、水厂分区送水、主管增压、高层建筑增压。
  • 工业增压：流程水系统、清洗系统、高压冲洗系统、消防系统。
  • 工业液体输送：冷却和空调系统、锅炉给水和冷凝系统、机床配套、酸性和碱性介质输送。
  • 水处理：超滤系统、反渗透系统、蒸馏系统、分离器、游泳池。
  • 灌溉：农田灌溉、喷灌、滴灌。

使用条件

  • 输送介质：稀薄、清洁、非易燃易爆、不含固体颗粒或纤维的液体。
  • 介质温度：常温型 -15℃ 至 +70℃，热水型 -15℃ 至 +120℃。
  • 环境温度：最高 +40℃。
  • 海拔高度：最高 1000m。

FAQ

What is the CDL / CDLF vertical multistage pump used for?

It is used for high-rise building water supply, boiler feed, industrial water treatment, firefighting systems, irrigation, pressure boosting and clean liquid transfer applications.

What is the difference between CDL and CDLF pumps?

CDL models are commonly used for general clean water applications, while CDLF models usually use stainless steel wet parts and are suitable for cleaner or light corrosive liquid transfer requirements.

Why choose a vertical multistage pump?

A vertical multistage pump can provide higher pressure and higher head while saving installation space, making it suitable for building and industrial systems.

How to choose the right CDL / CDLF pump model?

The model should be selected according to required flow rate, head, liquid type, working pressure, temperature, pipe diameter, voltage and installation environment.
