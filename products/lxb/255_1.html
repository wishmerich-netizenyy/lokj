Silent Permanent Magnet Shielded Booster Pump

LOKJ silent permanent magnet shielded booster pump is designed for household water pressure boosting, hot water circulation, indoor water systems and noise-sensitive installation environments.

The shielded water-cooled motor structure removes the traditional cooling fan, helping reduce operating noise while improving sealing performance and long-term reliability.

With permanent magnet motor technology, automatic water flow control and compact design, the SLT series provides quiet, efficient and convenient boosting for residential water applications.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 silent 系列永磁屏蔽增压泵，是一款专为解决家庭噪音痛点而设计的创新产品。不同于传统的风冷电机水泵，SLT系列采用屏蔽式水冷结构，利用流动的水流带走电机热量，从而取消了噪音较大的风扇部件。配合永磁转子技术，不仅运行声音低至人耳难以察觉，更拥有极高的能效比，零泄漏设计确保了家庭用水的安全与卫生。

产品特点

  • 极致静音：无风扇设计，水冷润滑，运行声音极低，适合室内安装。
  • 永磁高效：采用永磁电机，体积小、重量轻、效率高，比普通电机节能显著。
  • 绝对密封：屏蔽套将定子与转子隔离，无机械密封磨损，彻底杜绝漏水隐患。
  • 智能控制：内置水流开关，水龙头打开自动运行，关闭自动停止。
  • 免维护：水润滑轴承，无需加注润滑油，长期运行免维护。

应用领域

  • 家庭增压：公寓、住宅的自来水增压，特别是安装在厨房、卫生间等室内环境。
  • 热水循环：地暖、散热器系统的热水循环。
  • 设备配套：激光设备、焊接设备等需要静音冷却循环的场合。
  • 太阳能上水：辅助太阳能热水器上下水增压。

使用条件

  • 输送介质：稀薄、清洁、无腐蚀性、不含固体颗粒或纤维的液体。
  • 介质温度：+2℃ 至 +90℃（耐高温设计）。
  • 环境温度：不超过 +40℃。
  • 安装方式：电机轴必须水平安装，以保证轴承润滑。

FAQ

What is a silent shielded booster pump used for?

It is used for household water pressure boosting, hot water circulation, indoor water systems, solar water heater boosting and equipment cooling circulation.

Why is a shielded pump quieter than a traditional booster pump?

A shielded pump uses a water-cooled motor structure without a traditional cooling fan, which helps reduce operating noise during indoor use.

Is the SLT pump suitable for hot water circulation?

Yes. The SLT series can be used for hot water circulation systems, heating circulation and other clean liquid circulation applications within the suitable temperature range.

How should this pump be installed?

The motor shaft should be installed horizontally, and the pump should be used with clean liquid without solid particles or fibers to ensure stable operation.
