IRG / ISW Inline Centrifugal Pipeline Pump

LOKJ IRG / ISW inline centrifugal pipeline pump is designed for building water supply, HVAC circulation, industrial water transfer, fire boosting and pipeline pressure systems.

The inlet and outlet have the same diameter and are arranged on the same centerline, allowing the pump to be installed directly into the pipeline like a valve.

With stable operation, compact structure and convenient maintenance, the IRG / ISW series is suitable for high-rise buildings, factories, municipal water systems, garden irrigation and heating or cooling circulation.

产品详情

产品简介

绿欧科技 IRG/ISW 系列单级单吸管道离心泵，是本公司参照国际标准ISO2858和国家标准JB/T53058-93进行设计制造的高效节能产品。该系列水泵采用立式（IRG）或卧式（ISW）结构，叶轮直接安装在电机加长轴上，具有运行平稳、振动小、噪音低的特点。其进出口径相同且在同一水平线上，可直接安装在管道的任何位置，如同阀门一样方便。

产品特点

  • 安装便捷：进出口径相同且在同一中心线上，占地面积小，如加上防护罩可置于户外使用。
  • 运行平稳：叶轮直接安装在电机轴上，同心度高，运行平稳，振动极小。
  • 滴水不漏：轴封采用硬质合金机械密封，耐磨损，有效延长使用寿命，杜绝跑冒滴漏。
  • 维护方便：无需拆卸管道系统，只需卸下泵体螺母即可抽出全部转子部件进行检修。
  • 串并联使用：可根据流量和扬程的使用要求，采用泵的串、并联运行方式。

应用领域

  • 建筑增压：高层建筑增压送水、消防系统增压。
  • 暖通空调：冷热水循环、冷却塔供水、采暖系统循环。
  • 市政工程：城市给排水、远距离输送。
  • 园林灌溉：农田灌溉、园林喷灌。
  • 工业应用：工业循环系统、加工工艺供水。

使用条件

  • 吸入压力：≤1.0MPa，或泵系统最高工作压力≤1.6MPa。
  • 介质温度：普通型适用于 -15℃ 至 +80℃，热水型（IRG）可达 +120℃。
  • 环境温度：不超过 +40℃，相对湿度不超过 95%。
  • 介质要求：清水或物理化学性质类似于清水的液体，固体颗粒体积不超过单位体积的0.1%。

FAQ

What is the IRG / ISW inline centrifugal pump used for?

It is used for building water supply, high-rise pressure boosting, HVAC circulation, fire boosting, garden irrigation and industrial water transfer.

What is the difference between IRG and ISW pumps?

IRG is usually a vertical inline pipeline pump, while ISW is commonly used as a horizontal pipeline centrifugal pump. Both can be used for pipeline water transfer and circulation.

Can this pump be installed directly on the pipeline?

Yes. The inlet and outlet are on the same centerline, so the pump can be installed directly in the pipeline system with a compact installation layout.

How to choose the right IRG / ISW pump model?

The model should be selected according to flow rate, head, pipe diameter, working pressure, liquid temperature, installation method and application requirements.
